About Our Family

A Legacy Rooted in the Soil

Its story is grounded in the work, care, faith, and determination of generations who understood the value of protecting land and passing it forward.

Born on October 3, 1902 in Emanuel County, Georgia, Harry Baldwin was raised in a hardworking farming family and helped continue the agricultural traditions passed down through generations. His parents, Charlie Baldwin and Mattie Cotton Baldwin, lived and worked in the Swainsboro and Stillmore communities during the early 1900s.

The Baldwin family worked the land, raised crops, and contributed to the local agricultural economy during a time when land ownership was a significant achievement for African American families in the rural South.

Emily Baldwin

Emily Baldwin of Emanuel County, Georgia, was recognized as one of Georgia's top 4-H youth leaders in 1950. She earned state recognition at Savannah State College for excellence in agricultural leadership, project work, and community service.

Her dedication to the 4-H values of Head, Heart, Hands, and Health continues to inspire the mission of HMH Wellness & Farm today.

Current Generation

Continuing the Legacy

Heidi B. Fuller is the Founder & Chief Vision Officer of HMH Wellness & Farm, bringing more than 20 years of experience in brand strategy, community engagement, and corporate partnerships. Her vision connects a century-old family legacy in Emanuel County with modern wellness, education, and outdoor experiences.

Hunter F. Reed serves as Co-Founder & Visionary Director of Outdoor Leadership. A United States Navy veteran, Hunter leads outdoor programming, land stewardship, safety initiatives, and community outreach. Alongside Heidi, he has helped build HMH Wellness & Farm into a place for wellness retreats, executive retreats, veterans programs, youth outdoor experiences, camping, fishing, and family gatherings.

Madison B. White serves as Co-Founder & Director of Guest Experience. With extensive experience in customer service, scheduling, and administrative operations, Madison helps create welcoming experiences for guests while supporting program coordination and retreat operations.
